Open for the UI review

Q1 Low-HP emphasis

Callouts tag LOW at ≤35% (WSG-3). Should the band flip the HP% to red / add ! at that threshold so the kill window reads pre-attentively? Shown tinted above as a proposal.

Q2 HP as a mini-bar

A 3–4px threat-coloured bar under the EFC name vs. the bare 34%. Richer, but heavier than the native one-line band — worth it?

Q3 Empty-state clarity

Is our flag safe / their flag safe legible enough at a glance, or should base-state carry a subtle anchored marker?

Q4 Colour-blind safety

Red/blue leans on hue; the EFC/FFC label prefixes already disambiguate, but worth a deuteranopia check before calling it done.
